Overview

BAGO is an open-source bioinformatics tool designed for the systematic optimization of LC gradient using the Bayesian optimization framework. It is particularly useful for optimizing LC gradients in LC-MS-based metabolomics, lipidomics, and exposomics.

Also known as: Bayesian Optimization for LC Gradient Optimization
