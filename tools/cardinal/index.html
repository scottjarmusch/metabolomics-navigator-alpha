Overview

Cardinal provides reproducible analysis of mass spectrometry imaging experiments, including preprocessing, visualization, spatial segmentation, classification and multivariate analysis. Cardinal v3 supports a broad range of MSI workflows and is designed for programmatic analysis in R.

What can this tool do?

Multivariate statistics

Common uses

  • Preprocess and visualize MSI datasets.
  • Segment tissues by chemical composition.
  • Perform classification and multivariate analysis of MSI experiments.

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Reproducible statistical analysis of MALDI- and DESI-based MSI datasets.

Not designed for

  • Definitive metabolite identification solely from ion images or statistical classification.

Important requirements

  • Compatible imaging-MS data, spatial coordinates and an R/Bioconductor environment.
  • Biological sample metadata and an analysis design appropriate to spatial measurements.

Considerations

  • Pixels from one tissue are not independent biological replicates; preserve sample-level grouping during inference and validation.
  • Interpretation depends on acquisition quality, preprocessing choices and spatial experimental design.
