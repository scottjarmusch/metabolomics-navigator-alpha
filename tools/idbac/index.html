Overview

IDBac combines bacterial MALDI protein fingerprints with specialized-metabolite profiles to compare cultured isolates. Its web platform provides reference matching, protein-similarity views and metabolite-association analysis. This entry covers the current platform and its earlier R/desktop methods; older installation instructions are not interchangeable with the current GNPS2 interface.

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • High-throughput analysis of cultured bacterial isolates using MALDI-TOF or compatible MALDI mass-spectrometry data.
  • Connecting taxonomic or protein-fingerprint similarity with differences in specialized-metabolite production.
  • Culture-collection dereplication and strain prioritization before labor-intensive scale-up.

Not designed for

  • Replacing sequence-based taxonomy when definitive taxonomic resolution is required.
  • Complete structural elucidation of specialized metabolites from MALDI-MS1 profiles alone.

Important requirements

  • Reproducible MALDI sample preparation and appropriate replicate acquisition.
  • Protein-region spectra for bacterial grouping or identification and, where chemical comparison is desired, lower-mass specialized-metabolite spectra.

Considerations

  • Protein spectral similarity is not definitive sequence-based taxonomy.
  • Culture conditions, MALDI matrix and acquisition settings affect detected chemical profiles.
  • MS1 metabolite-association networks do not establish structures or imply MS/MS fragmentation similarity.

Evidence and review

Official project documentation and direct tool publication metadata reviewed on 2026-09-12. No account creation, data upload, installation or independent benchmarking performed. Included for MALDI specialized-metabolite profiling and culture-collection interpretation. The separate colony MALDI-MS/MS method paper is not listed as a core platform publication.
