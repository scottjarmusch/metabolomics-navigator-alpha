Overview

MS-DIAL is a metabolomics and lipidomics workbench designed for broad-spectrum processing of chromatography–mass-spectrometry datasets. It combines peak detection, deconvolution, alignment, spectral annotation, normalization, statistics, and visualization, with particular support for data-independent MS/MS processing and lipid annotation.

Also known as: MSDIAL, MS Dial

What can this tool do?

Peak or feature detectionSpectral deconvolutionRetention-time alignmentGap fillingAdduct and isotope groupingSpectral-library matchingMS/MS processingMultivariate statistics

Common uses

  • Process untargeted LC-MS, GC-MS, and lipidomics datasets through a graphical workflow.
  • Deconvolute data-independent MS/MS spectra and match features against spectral libraries.
  • Export aligned abundances and representative spectra for downstream analysis while preserving feature IDs.

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Broad untargeted metabolomics and lipidomics processing through a graphical workflow across several chromatography and MS acquisition modes.
  • Datasets requiring deconvolution and interpretation of data-independent or all-ion MS/MS signals.

Not designed for

  • Treating all library annotations as equally resolved molecular structures.

Important requirements

  • Users should confirm current operating-system support and input-format compatibility for the version they install.

Considerations

  • Large libraries and highly permissive annotation settings can increase candidate identifications; confidence levels and library provenance should be reported.
  • For lipidomics, report the structural level supported by the fragmentation evidence; review deconvolution and retention information for ambiguous features.
