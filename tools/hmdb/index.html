Overview

The Human Metabolome Database links compound records to biological context and experimental or predicted analytical information. Evidence types vary across entries.

What can this tool do?

Structure-database searching

Common uses

  • Look up synonyms and structures for human metabolites.
  • Review available spectral and biological evidence for an annotation.

Other, Metabolite records and selected downloads

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Look up synonyms and structures for human metabolites.

Not designed for

  • Assuming every listed compound has been experimentally measured in the study population.

Important requirements

  • A compound name, identifier, structure or compatible analytical query.

Considerations

  • Distinguish measured information from predictions and expected metabolites; inspect the source supporting each field.
