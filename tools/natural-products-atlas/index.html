Overview

The Natural Products Atlas links microbial natural products to origins and references, with tools for structural and chemical-space exploration.

What can this tool do?

Structure-database searching

Common uses

  • Find related microbial natural-product scaffolds.
  • Trace a reported compound back to its isolation literature.

Output formats

Other, Structures, origin records and references

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Find related microbial natural-product scaffolds.

Not designed for

  • A complete reference for all plant, animal or synthetic compounds.

Important requirements

  • A compound name, molecular property or structure-based search.

Considerations

  • Reported origin is contextual evidence; it does not establish that a compound in a new sample came from the same organism.
