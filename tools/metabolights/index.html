Overview

MetaboLights is the EMBL-EBI repository for metabolomics experiments across organisms and analytical techniques. Study records connect raw and derived files to sample and assay descriptions, supporting publication-associated sharing and later reuse.

What can this tool do?

Metadata-based contextualizationProvenance tracking

Common uses

  • Deposit the files and metadata supporting a metabolomics publication.
  • Find studies by organism or analytical technology for reanalysis.

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Structured sharing of complete metabolomics experiments, with this entry focused on MS-based studies.

Not designed for

  • Automatic harmonization of measurements across unrelated studies.

Important requirements

  • Prepare sample-to-file mappings, assay details and metabolite identification tables following the submission guide.

Considerations

  • Inspect study design and processing history before combining datasets. Record the MTBLS accession and cite the original study as well as the repository.
