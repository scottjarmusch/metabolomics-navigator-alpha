Overview

MassIVE is a community repository for the public exchange of mass-spectrometry data. It stores raw and processed files, metadata and publication-associated datasets, and supports browsing, downloading, accessioned deposition, and reuse through GNPS and related services.

Also known as: Mass Spectrometry Interactive Virtual Environment

What can this tool do?

Repository-scale spectral searchProvenance tracking

Common uses

  • Deposit publication-associated mass-spectrometry datasets.
  • Browse and download public mass-spectrometry data for reuse and reanalysis.
  • Deposit a publication-associated dataset and provide its accession so others can download and reanalyze it.

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Long-term deposition and public reuse of mass-spectrometry datasets.

Not designed for

  • Automatic validation of metabolite identities or a substitute for sample-level experimental documentation.

Important requirements

  • Data and metadata must be uploaded and a dataset explicitly made public when public access is intended.

Considerations

  • Dataset-level metadata quality affects downstream discoverability and reanalysis.
  • Keep raw data, processed files and a sample-to-file mapping together; public deposition alone does not imply that the data are analysis-ready.
