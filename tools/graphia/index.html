Overview

Graphia imports graphs or builds correlation networks from numerical tables, with clustering and interactive 2D or 3D views. It is a general visual-analysis environment included here for downstream MS metabolomics, rather than a mass-spectrometry processing engine.

What can this tool do?

Network visualizationInteractive dashboard

Common uses

  • Explore feature-correlation networks after preprocessing.
  • Cluster and compare annotated graph components.

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • General graph analysis and visual exploration of numerical-data relationships.

Not designed for

  • MS/MS matching, raw-data processing or inference of causal biochemical interactions.

Important requirements

  • A cleaned numerical matrix or an existing graph with meaningful identifiers.

Considerations

  • Normalization, missing values, batch effects and correlation thresholds influence network structure.
  • Graph clusters are exploratory patterns and need independent biological assessment.
