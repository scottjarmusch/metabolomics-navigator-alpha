Overview

FooDB links compounds with foods and literature-derived information, helping researchers contextualize potential dietary metabolites.

What can this tool do?

Structure-database searching

Common uses

  • Investigate possible food sources for a candidate metabolite.
  • Cross-reference food compounds with other chemical databases.

Primary function

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Investigate possible food sources for a candidate metabolite.

Not designed for

  • Assigning a unique dietary source from a compound match.

Important requirements

  • A food, compound name or compatible chemical query.

Considerations

  • Food composition depends on processing and biological variation; inspect measurement sources before making exposure inferences.
