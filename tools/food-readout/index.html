Overview

Food Readout joins GNPS library matches to food metadata and ontology categories. It offers feature-level and group-level views of candidate food-associated signals, including abundance plots and exploratory statistical displays.

What can this tool do?

Metadata-based contextualizationInteractive dashboard

Common uses

  • Explore food-associated annotations in nutrition or exposome studies.
  • Inspect variation in candidate dietary markers across sample groups.

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Post-processing LC-MS/MS library matches in their food-related reference context.

Not designed for

  • Measuring absolute food intake or proving recent consumption.

Important requirements

  • A completed FBMN task with GNPS library matches, a feature quantification table and sample metadata.
  • CSV/TSV tables with matching feature and sample identifiers.

Considerations

  • Reference coverage is incomplete, and many compounds occur in food and non-food sources.
  • Group differences require study-design and statistical review; an exploratory plot is not causal evidence.

Access and documentation

Primary access↗Documentation↗

Publicly hosted MetaboApp. GNPS2 infrastructure is open to the academic community; its documentation directs industry users to the maintainers or UCR Technology Transfer. Hosted access does not establish a software or reference-data license.
