Overview

peakPantheR searches specified retention-time and m/z regions, fits chromatographic peaks and reports integration results with diagnostic plots. It supports processing many files in parallel and reviewing parameters through a local Shiny interface. The target list supplies the expected feature annotations; extraction does not independently establish compound identity.

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Targeted extraction from predefined retention-time and m/z regions in profiling data.

Not designed for

  • Discovery of all unknown features or de novo metabolite identification.

Important requirements

  • A target annotation table with feature identifiers and retention-time/mass windows.
  • MS files supported by the underlying R readers and a compatible R/Bioconductor installation.

Considerations

  • Chromatographic drift and coelution can compromise extraction within fixed windows.
  • Inspect fitting diagnostics and retain target definitions and parameter changes.
  • A fitted peak does not replace reference-standard evidence for its assigned identity.
