Overview

SpaMTP combines spatial metabolomics and transcriptomics analysis in an R environment built around Seurat objects and Cardinal functionality. It supports m/z-based candidate annotation, statistical comparison and pathway analysis, alongside visualization and multimodal integration.

What can this tool do?

Univariate statisticsMultivariate statisticsPathway enrichmentContainer support

Common uses

  • Compare spatial metabolite patterns and associated pathways.
  • Integrate spatial MS measurements with transcriptomic context.

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Joint spatial metabolomics and transcriptomics analysis.

Not designed for

  • Treating m/z-only candidate annotation as definitive identification.

Important requirements

  • R and documented package dependencies, or the supplied container environment.
  • Appropriate spatial coordinates and compatible inputs for the chosen workflow.

Considerations

  • Annotation ambiguity propagates to pathway interpretation.
  • Alignment and statistical comparisons depend on sampling geometry and biological replication.
  • Record the branch, package version and reference databases used.
