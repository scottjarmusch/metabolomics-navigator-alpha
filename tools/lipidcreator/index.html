Overview

LipidCreator calculates lipid precursor and fragment masses from user-defined lipid building blocks. It supports isotope-labelled targets and predicted spectral libraries, with direct Skyline integration or standalone use.

What can this tool do?

In-silico spectrum prediction

Common uses

  • Prepare lipid transition lists for targeted acquisition.
  • Generate predicted lipid fragment libraries for Skyline workflows.

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Assay design for supported lipid classes.

Not designed for

  • Automatic proof that predicted transitions are selective in a biological matrix.

Important requirements

  • Target lipid definitions and compatible runtime; Skyline for integrated workflows.

Considerations

  • Validate transition selectivity and collision energies experimentally for the intended instrument.
