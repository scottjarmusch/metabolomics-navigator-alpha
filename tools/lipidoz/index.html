Overview

LipidOz scores isotope-distribution evidence for candidate OzID fragments and provides a graphical review interface. Its documented workflows include LC and infusion analyses; ordinary collision-induced MS/MS is not a substitute for OzID measurements.

What can this tool do?

Structure elucidation

Common uses

  • Score candidate lipid double-bond positions from OzID fragments.
  • Review fragment evidence and export target-level scoring reports.

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Structural interpretation of compatible OzID lipid measurements.

Not designed for

  • General lipid discovery without OzID data.

Important requirements

  • OzID data, a compatible target list and workflow-specific extraction settings. The LC guide uses MZA data and CSV lipid/adduct/retention-time targets.

Considerations

  • Inspect diagnostic-fragment quality and ambiguous assignments; a software score alone is not structural confirmation.
