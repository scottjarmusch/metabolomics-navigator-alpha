What does this strategy enable?

MS2Planner uses topological sorting on a feature graph to maximize scheduled coverage. Features selected for one path are excluded from the next planning iteration. This is computational scheduling of repeat acquisitions, rather than a requirement to measure fragmentation quality after every run.

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Increasing MS/MS coverage in untargeted LC-MS experiments when repeat injections are practical.
  • Building richer fragmentation coverage for annotation and discovery from representative or pooled samples.

Important requirements

  • Full-scan survey features and instrument support for the selected inclusion-list acquisition mode.
  • Sufficient sample for repeated injections and chromatography consistent with the planned retention-time windows.

Considerations

  • Planning assumptions and acquisition timing must fit the instrument and chromatography.
  • This is not equivalent to a simple exclusion list updated after each measured DDA run; no universal coverage gain is promised.
