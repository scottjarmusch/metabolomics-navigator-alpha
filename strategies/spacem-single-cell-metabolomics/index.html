What does this strategy enable?

SpaceM combines light microscopy, image segmentation, MALDI mass spectrometry imaging, and spatial registration to convert tissue- or culture-level ion images into per-cell metabolic profiles. The key methodological step is aligning optical cell information with MSI ablation coordinates so metabolite signals can be associated with individual cells while retaining morpho-spatial context.

Transferable workflow

Workflow

  1. 1

    Acquire microscopy of the same cells

    Record optical images that preserve cell morphology and spatial context before MALDI analysis.

  2. 2

    Segment individual cells

    Derive cell boundaries and image-derived features from the optical data.

  3. 3

    Acquire MALDI mass spectrometry imaging

    Collect spatially resolved metabolite signals across the same cellular field.

  4. 4

    Register optical and MSI coordinate systems

    Align cell masks with MALDI ablation locations so mass-spectral signals can be assigned to individual cells.

  5. 5

    Construct per-cell metabolic profiles

    Aggregate registered ion signals into a cell-by-metabolite matrix while retaining morphologic and spatial information.

  6. 6

    Identify metabolic cell states

    Analyze single-cell metabolic profiles to distinguish heterogeneous cell states and relate them to morphology or experimental conditions.

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • In situ metabolic profiling of heterogeneous cell populations at single-cell resolution.
  • Linking metabolic states to cell morphology and spatial position.

Important requirements

  • Compatible microscopy, cell segmentation, and MALDI-MSI measurements from the same spatial field.
  • Accurate spatial registration between optical images and MSI coordinates.

Considerations

  • Cell-level profiles depend on segmentation and registration accuracy.
  • MALDI pixel size, analyte delocalization, and ion suppression can influence per-cell assignments.
