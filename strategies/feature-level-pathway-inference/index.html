What does this strategy enable?

The mummichog method connects ambiguous mass-to-metabolite matches to metabolic-network structure. Enrichment is evaluated against the measured feature background, allowing collective evidence to prioritize biological hypotheses for experimental follow-up.

Transferable workflow

Workflow

  1. 1

    Define significant and background features

    Prepare significant feature masses and the full measured feature list from the comparison.

  2. 2

    Map candidate metabolites to a network

    Use mass accuracy, ionization assumptions and a suitable metabolic model to retain plausible matches.

    mummichog
  3. 3

    Evaluate network and pathway enrichment

    Compare observed enrichment with permutations from the feature background.

    mummichog
  4. 4

    Validate prioritized hypotheses

    Follow up selected metabolites and pathways with independent measurements.

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Functional interpretation when many significant features remain unidentified.

Important requirements

  • Feature statistics, measured background and a relevant metabolic model.

Considerations

  • Enrichment is a hypothesis, not confirmation of individual identities or a measurement of flux.
  • Background selection and ambiguous ion assignments influence results.
