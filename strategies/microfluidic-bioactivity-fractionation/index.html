What does this strategy enable?

Pair rapid chromatographic fraction collection with LC-MS/MS and luminescent bioreporters to connect chemical signals with antibacterial responses.

Transferable workflow

Workflow

  1. 1

    Collect fractions during LC-MS/MS

    Spot eluent onto a paper microfluidic device at approximately one fraction per second while acquiring spectra.

  2. 2

    Measure reporter responses

    Expose fractions to luminescent bioreporters and image the response.

  3. 3

    Align activity with chemistry

    Match reporter signals to chromatographic profiles to prioritize active compounds.

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Antibacterial activity screening of extracts or compound mixtures.

Important requirements

  • Custom rapid fractionation hardware and compatible paper devices.
  • LC-MS/MS, luminescent bioreporters and response imaging.
  • Reliable correspondence between fraction positions and retention times.

Considerations

  • Reporter responses are assay-specific; confirm prioritized activity with follow-up experiments.
  • Coelution can complicate assigning activity to one compound.
