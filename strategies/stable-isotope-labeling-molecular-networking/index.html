What does this strategy enable?

Stable-isotope labeling–assisted molecular networking layers biosynthetic incorporation information onto MS/MS similarity. A labeled precursor such as a stable-isotope amino acid is supplied to a producing organism, labeled mass shifts are detected in metabolite features, and molecular networking groups related MS/MS spectra. The combination helps distinguish biosynthetically connected analogues and can provide sequence or building-block information that molecular networking alone does not supply.

Transferable workflow

Workflow

  1. 1

    Select and feed a stable-isotope precursor

    Add a labeled biosynthetic building block to the culture under conditions that permit incorporation into specialized metabolites, while retaining appropriate unlabeled controls.

  2. 2

    Detect isotope incorporation

    Compare labeled and unlabeled LC-MS data to identify features with mass shifts consistent with incorporation of the supplied precursor.

  3. 3

    Acquire and compare MS/MS spectra

    Collect fragmentation spectra for candidate labeled and related metabolites.

  4. 4

    Build a molecular network

    Organize structurally related spectra by MS/MS similarity and overlay isotope-incorporation evidence to recognize families sharing the labeled building block.

    GNPS
  5. 5

    Infer biosynthetic or structural relationships

    Use the combined label pattern and fragmentation relationships to prioritize analogues, infer building-block composition, and guide targeted structural validation.

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Linking natural-product analogues to a supplied biosynthetic precursor.
  • Characterizing families of peptide or other metabolites where isotope incorporation provides interpretable biosynthetic information.

Important requirements

  • A biologically plausible labeled precursor and culture system capable of incorporating it.
  • Matched labeled and unlabeled controls and sufficient mass accuracy to resolve the expected isotope shift.

Considerations

  • Label incorporation may be incomplete, diluted by metabolism, or redistributed into downstream metabolites.
  • Molecular similarity and label incorporation support a biosynthetic relationship but do not by themselves establish complete pathway genetics or final structure.
