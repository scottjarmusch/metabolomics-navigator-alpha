What does this strategy enable?

Differential dansylation couples selective chemical tagging with coeluting isotope pairs. Sample-to-reference signal ratios support comparative quantification; labeled standards enable calibrated measurements.

Transferable workflow

Workflow

  1. 1

    Label samples and reference

    React compatible metabolites with light or heavy dansyl reagents.

  2. 2

    Mix labeled material

    Combine labeled sample with reference material or calibrated standards.

  3. 3

    Separate and measure isotope pairs

    Analyze coeluting pairs by LC-MS.

  4. 4

    Calculate comparative abundances

    Use pair ratios and appropriate calibration for the intended measurement.

Strategy profile

Components

Sample preparation, Chromatography, Data acquisition, Statistical analysis

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Comparative profiling of chemically reactive amines and phenols.

Important requirements

  • Compatible derivatization chemistry, labeled reference material and LC-MS.

Considerations

  • Chemical isotope tags do not measure biological isotope incorporation or metabolic flux.
  • Coverage is restricted by reactive functional groups; isotope pairs alone do not confirm structure.
