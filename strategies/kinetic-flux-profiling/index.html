What does this strategy enable?

Kinetic flux profiling follows replacement of unlabeled metabolites after switching a nutrient source to its isotope-labeled equivalent. The original demonstration used nitrogen assimilation in cultured E. coli; the transferable contribution is the link between labeling dynamics, pool sizes and flux.

Transferable workflow

Workflow

  1. 1

    Switch nutrient isotope labeling

    Change the isotopic nutrient source while preserving the intended culture conditions.

  2. 2

    Sample the labeling time course

    Quench and extract replicate samples at times that resolve metabolite turnover.

  3. 3

    Measure labeling and pool sizes

    Use chromatography-MS to quantify unlabeled fractions and intracellular metabolite concentrations.

  4. 4

    Fit the specified kinetic model

    Estimate turnover and flux using equations appropriate to precursor labeling and pathway structure.

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Flux estimation in cultured cells with experimentally resolved labeling kinetics.

Important requirements

  • Rapid nutrient switching, timed quenching, isotope-resolved measurements and pool-size quantification.

Considerations

  • Basic interpretation assumes metabolic steady state; dynamic variants require additional modeling.
  • Reliable quantification requires resolving turnover relative to upstream precursor labeling.
  • A single endpoint or labeling half-time without pool-size and model context does not establish absolute flux.
