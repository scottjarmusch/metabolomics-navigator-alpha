What does this strategy enable?

This method links spatial metabolite signals to immunophenotyped cells on the same tissue section, adding a molecular cell-identity layer beyond morphology.

Transferable workflow

Workflow

  1. 1

    Acquire MALDI images

    Measure metabolites before antibody staining.

  2. 2

    Acquire immunophenotypes

    Remove matrix, stain with metal-tagged antibodies and perform IMC.

  3. 3

    Register images and segment cells

    Align modalities and derive cellular boundaries and phenotypes.

  4. 4

    Assign metabolite signals

    Aggregate overlapping image signals into relative per-cell profiles.

Components

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Spatial comparison of immunophenotyped cell populations.

Important requirements

  • Compatible same-section MSI and IMC measurements.

Considerations

  • MSI pixels can overlap multiple cells, mixing assigned signals.
  • Cellular attribution depends on registration and segmentation.
