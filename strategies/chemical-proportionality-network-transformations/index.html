What does this strategy enable?

Combine molecular-network edges with abundance changes across ordered samples to prioritize possible chemical transformations.

Transferable workflow

Workflow

  1. 1

    Prepare matched network and abundance data

    Connect quantified features to fragmentation-based network edges and ordered sample metadata.

  2. 2

    Score proportional changes

    Apply the appropriate ChemProp scoring mode to connected feature pairs.

    Chemical Proportionality (ChemProp)
  3. 3

    Investigate candidate transformations

    Inspect abundance trends, mass differences and spectra before follow-up validation.

Strategy profile

Components

Molecular networking, Metadata analysis, Statistical analysis

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Prioritizing possible transformations among network-connected features.

Important requirements

  • MS/MS network edges linked to quantified features.
  • Consistent sample identifiers and an ordered temporal or spatial variable.
  • Choose the scoring mode for the available sampling design.

Considerations

  • Direction and proportionality are hypotheses, not proof of a reaction or its mechanism.
  • Co-variation, normalization and missing signals affect interpretation.
  • This does not estimate metabolic flux.
