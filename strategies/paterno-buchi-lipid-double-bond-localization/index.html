What does this strategy enable?

The defining approach converts unsaturated lipids into oxetane reaction products. Collision-induced fragmentation of those products yields diagnostic ions that report double-bond position.

Transferable workflow

Workflow

  1. 1

    Photochemically modify lipid double bonds

    The original implementation irradiates a nanoelectrospray plume containing lipids and acetone with UV light.

  2. 2

    Fragment reaction products

    Acquire tandem mass spectra of the derivatized lipids using collision activation.

  3. 3

    Interpret diagnostic fragments

    Assign candidate double-bond locations from the oxetane-cleavage products.

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Locating double bonds in unsaturated lipids.

Important requirements

  • A compatible UV photoreaction and tandem-MS setup.

Considerations

  • This is photochemical derivatization, distinct from gas-phase ozone-induced dissociation.
  • Do not assume that software written for OzID interprets these spectra.
