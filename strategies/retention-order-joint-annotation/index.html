What does this strategy enable?

The 2018 method integrates learned pairwise retention preferences with spectral evidence rather than filtering each feature independently by absolute retention time.

Transferable workflow

Workflow

  1. 1

    Prepare candidate lists

    Associate measured retention times with spectra and candidate structures.

  2. 2

    Score spectral candidates

    The original study uses IOKR scores.

    IOKR
  3. 3

    Learn and integrate retention preferences

    Combine RankSVM order predictions and spectral scores using the published dynamic-programming procedure.

  4. 4

    Review ranked assignments

    Inspect candidates and seek independent confirmation.

Extension · Nature Machine Intelligence · 2022

Joint structural annotation of small molecules using liquid chromatography retention order and tandem mass spectrometry data

LC-MS2Struct learns structured integration of spectral scores and retention order, including stereochemistry-aware fingerprints. This replaces the original integration model rather than reproducing its algorithm.

doi:10.1038/s42256-022-00577-2

Strategy profile

Components

Chromatography, Annotation or identification, Statistical analysis

Scope and considerations

Designed for

  • Joint interpretation of LC-MS/MS features.

Important requirements

  • Candidate structures, spectral scores, measured retention order and suitable training data.

Considerations

  • Candidate coverage and chromatographic transferability matter. Rankings do not confirm identities.
