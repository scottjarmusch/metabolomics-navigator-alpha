Inspecting and interpreting results

Inspect signals and interpret study results.

How to choose a resource

Once data have been processed, researchers need to inspect signals, analyze variation across samples, and interpret results in biological context. An interactive viewer, a statistical platform, and a collection of downstream applications provide different kinds of support.

Start with your study design and the form of your input data. Preserve sample identifiers, preprocessing decisions, and analysis settings when moving between tools.
