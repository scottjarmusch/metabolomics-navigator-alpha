Guide›Spatial metabolomics

Working with spatial metabolomics

Explore molecular distributions in tissues and surfaces.

How to choose a resource

Imaging mass spectrometry links molecular signals to spatial coordinates. Processing, visualization and annotation address different questions; an ion image alone does not establish molecular identity.

Start with the acquisition mode, spatial coordinates and available file formats. Keep biological replication distinct from the number of measured pixels.
